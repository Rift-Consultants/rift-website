Layered background recipe

Dark surface, soft radial glow, subtle grain.

This example recreates the Framer-style look: a near-black base, low-opacity radial lighting, an SVG noise overlay, translucent cards, faint borders, and blur.

Primary action Secondary action
Base #0a0a0a page and section background.
Glow White radial gradients at very low opacity.
Texture The visible texture comes from the ::before noise overlay, not the radial gradient alone.
Cards rgba(255,255,255,.02-.05) surfaces.
Stroke Very faint borders, blur, and masked highlights.
